I am a 5th year physics graduate student at the University of Florida. Having obtained my Bachelors at the University of Crete, I came to UF to pursue a doctorate in cosmology.

Throughout my PhD, I have worked on two different research directions: topological defect phenomenology and inflation. On the inflation side, I am interested in studying the evolution of spectator fields during inflation and how their interactions can be intepreted stochastically. I have brought to completion two projects: 

- **Large Inflationary Logarithms in a Nontrivial Nonlinear Sigma Model**: My collaborators and I tested an extension of Starobinskys Stochastic Formalism for scalar fields with derivative interactions in a non-linear sigma model. In particular, we cross checked the leading logarithm predictions of the stochastic formalism with their direct two-loop counterpart, finding exact agreement between the two.

- **Sensing the Inflationary Production of Scalars**: My collaborators and I investigated the effects of loop corrections to the graviton self energy from conformally coupled fields and massless, minimally coupled scalars and interpreted them using the stochastic formalism and a variant of the renormalization group.

On the topological defect side, I am interested in observational signatures of cosmic string networks, most notably how to capture them using analytical methods. I have brought to completion two projects:

- **Gravitational Waves from Long Strings and Loops**: My collaborators and I developed a semi-analytical framework that (1) analytically tracks the network on large scales and (2) infers small-scale behavior from simulations. This framework was validated by computing the gravitational wave signal of long gauge strings, which reproduced simulated results to remarkable accuracy, and subsequently applied to long global strings and loops, obtaining for the first time the full intensity spectrum across all frequencies.

- **Axion Strings from String Axions**: My collaborators and I proposed a novel formation mechanism that proceeds via a first-order Randall-Sundrum compactification phase transition: strings form at the junction of three bubbles during percolation, and the resulting tension is set by the warp factor, making it parametrically smaller than that of field-theory axion strings.
